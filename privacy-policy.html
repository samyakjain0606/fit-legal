Privacy Policy for fit.

Last updated: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the fit. mobile application ("fit.", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our Android application (the "App"). By using fit., you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

fit. is operated by the developer team behind the fit. virtual try-on application. For questions about this policy, contact us at samyakjain269@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Phone number — used solely for one-time-password (OTP) authentication via our authentication provider (Supabase). We do not send marketing messages.
  • Photos of yourself — uploaded so the App can generate virtual try-on images.
  • Photos and URLs of garments — provided by you (camera roll uploads or e-commerce product links such as Myntra, Ajio) so the App can fetch the garment image.
  • Try-on results — images generated by combining your photo and a garment.
  • Poll responses — if you create or vote in a friend poll, your vote and (optionally) your name are stored to render results.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate region.
  • Usage analytics — screen views, button taps, and feature engagement, collected via Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash and performance data — collected via Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose stability issues.
  • Network logs — minimal request metadata (timestamps, response codes) for debugging.

We do not collect: precise GPS location, contacts, calendar, SMS content, microphone audio, or browsing history outside the App.

3. How we use your information

  • Authenticate your account via phone OTP.
  • Generate virtual try-on images by sending your photo and the garment image to our backend AI service.
  • Fetch garment images from product URLs you submit.
  • Host friend polls so others can vote on your try-on results.
  • Operate, maintain, debug, and improve the App.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and policy violations.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your photos to train machine-learning models. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Photo retention and deletion

All photos you upload (your selfie, garment images, try-on results) are automatically deleted from our servers 30 days after upload.

You may also delete your photos at any time from within the App, or by emailing samyakjain269@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".

6. Friend polls and sharing

If you create a friend poll, the poll's try-on images and the question you wrote are accessible to anyone with the poll's share link, until the poll is deleted or the 30-day retention expires. Voters submit anonymous or named votes; you can see vote tallies.

7. Children's privacy

fit. is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable threshold). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact samyakjain269@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use HTTPS for all network communication, authenticated session tokens (JWT) for API access, and presigned URLs for direct-to-storage uploads. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is perfectly secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data.
  • Withdraw consent and stop using the App.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email samyakjain269@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in regions where our service providers operate (including the United States, the European Union, and India). Where required, we rely on standard contractual safeguards.
