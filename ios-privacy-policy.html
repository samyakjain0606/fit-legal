Privacy Policy for fit. (iOS)

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the fit. mobile application ("fit.", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our iOS application (the "App"). By using fit., you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

fit. is operated by the developer team behind the fit. virtual try-on application. For questions about this policy, contact us at samyakjain269@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Email address — used to create your account and sign you in via our authentication provider (Supabase). If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, the account email (or Apple's private relay address, if you choose to hide your email) is shared with us by the provider for the same purpose. We do not send marketing messages.
  • Name — if your Apple or Google account shares a name at sign-in, or if you enter a display name in settings, it is stored to personalize your account.
  • Photos of yourself — uploaded so the App can generate virtual try-on images. Because these are ordinary photographs of you, they typically show your face and body.
  • Photos and URLs of garments — provided by you (photo library uploads, camera captures, or e-commerce product links such as Myntra or Ajio) so the App can obtain the garment image.
  • Try-on results — images generated by combining your photo and a garment, stored so your wardrobe is there when you return.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Network logs — minimal request metadata (timestamps, response codes, IP address) is logged by our backend hosting provider for short-term operations and abuse prevention. The App contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

We do not collect: precise or coarse location, contacts, calendar, health data, financial or payment information, microphone audio, browsing history outside the App, or any advertising identifier. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How we use your information

  • Authenticate your account via Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or email and password.
  • Generate virtual try-on images by sending your photo and the garment image to an AI image-generation provider (see Section 6), after you have given your permission in the App.
  • Obtain garment images from product URLs you submit.
  • Store your generated try-ons so your wardrobe persists across sessions.
  • Send you a local notification on your device when a try-on you started finishes (only if you grant notification permission).
  • Operate, maintain, debug, and improve the App.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and policy violations.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your photos to train machine-learning models. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not use it for tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

4. Face data

fit. does not collect biometric or face-geometry data. The App performs no face scanning, face tracking, or facial recognition (it does not use ARKit face tracking or any comparable technology), and we never use your photos to identify you or anyone else. The only face data we handle is the faces visible in the ordinary photographs you choose to upload, and in the try-on images generated from them.

  • Collection: photographs of you are collected only when you choose to upload them, via the iOS photo picker or camera.
  • Use: these photographs are used solely to render your virtual try-on images. They are not used for identification, advertising, analytics, or model training.
  • Sharing: when you request a try-on, and only after you have given your permission in the App, your photo and the garment image are transmitted to our AI image-generation provider (OpenAI, or Google Gemini as a fallback) solely to produce the try-on image. No name, email address, or account identifier is sent with them. See Section 6 for provider terms.
  • Storage: your photographs and try-on images are stored in private storage buckets with our storage provider (Supabase) and are transmitted encrypted. They are never public and are accessible only from your account.
  • Retention: your photographs and try-on images are kept until you delete them or delete your account, as described in Section 5.

5. Photo retention and deletion

Photos you upload (your selfie, garment images, and AI-generated try-on results) remain stored on our servers until you delete them or delete your account. You can delete any individual photo or look at any time from inside the App. You can delete your entire account from the in-app settings sheet or by emailing samyakjain269@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my fit. account".

When you request account deletion, all account-linked photos and try-on records are removed from our active systems within 30 days. Anonymous, aggregated metrics that do not identify you may be retained indefinitely.

For details on what is deleted and what may be briefly retained, see our Delete your account page.

7. Sharing try-on images

You can share an AI try-on image from the App by tapping the share button, which opens the iOS share sheet (for example, Messages or WhatsApp). When you choose a destination, that app or service receives the image and handles it under its own privacy policy. fit. itself does not host a public gallery of your try-on images.

8. Children's privacy

fit. is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable threshold). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact samyakjain269@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Security

All network communication uses HTTPS and standard iOS App Transport Security. We use authenticated session tokens (JWT) for API access and presigned URLs for direct-to-storage uploads. The App uses only standard encryption exempt from U.S. export documentation requirements. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is perfectly secure.

  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data.
  • Withdraw consent and stop using the App.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email samyakjain269@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

11. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in regions where our service providers operate (including the United States, the European Union, and India). Where required, we rely on standard contractual safeguards.
